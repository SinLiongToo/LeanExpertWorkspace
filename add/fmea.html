4. Risk Analysis

Severity

  • 1 = No effect
  • 2-3 = Minor
  • 4-5 = Moderate
  • 6-7 = High
  • 8 = Very High
  • 9 = Hazardous with warning
  • 10 = Hazardous without warning

Occurrence

  • 1 = Remote
  • 10 = Almost inevitable

Detection

  • 1 = Almost certain detection
  • 10 = Cannot detect
